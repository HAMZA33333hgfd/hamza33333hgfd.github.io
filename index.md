## 🎨 Customization
After opening your site, you may want to customize it to fit your needs. Here’s how to do that:

- **Edit Text**: Open the `index.html` file in any text editor (like Notepad or Visual Studio Code) and change the text between the `<h1>` or `<p>` tags to your desired content.
- **Change Colors**: Locate the CSS file in the folder. Look for color properties. Change the color codes to your preferred colors.
- **Add Images**: Put your images in the folder and update the `<img>` tags in the HTML file to link to your images.

## 🧐 Tips for Success
